# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the long-term technical direction for the solution, balancing product goals with maintainability, scalability, and system integrity. They help the team make sound architectural decisions and reduce avoidable rework.

### Responsibilities
- Define system design direction and key technical standards
- Review architecture and integration patterns across services or systems
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and trade-offs
- Support design reviews and technical decision-making
- Ensure systems remain maintainable, secure, and aligned to platform strategy

### Goals
- Build scalable, resilient, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architectural ambiguity
- Align technical delivery with product and business goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Cross-team planning on dependencies and integration points
- Technical guidance for engineering and PM decision-making

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate product goals into feasible technical approaches
- Partners with Developers to validate design choices and maintain quality standards
- Collaborates with Project Managers on roadmap dependencies, technical sequencing, and risk management

---

## Engineering Managers / Team Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Team Leads help teams deliver effectively by managing day-to-day execution, capacity, and team health while supporting technical and operational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity and prioritize work across delivery commitments
- Support sprint planning, execution, and delivery forecasting
- Resolve blockers, dependencies, and cross-team coordination issues
- Coach engineers and support growth, quality, and team effectiveness
- Track team health, workload balance, and delivery risk

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery and sustainable team performance
- Improve execution quality and reduce operational bottlenecks
- Support healthy, empowered engineering teams

### Typical Communication
- Team standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Delivery status updates with PMs and stakeholders
- Performance and workload discussions with engineering leaders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to align delivery schedules and priorities
- Helps Developers translate roadmap items into actionable work and remove blockers
- Coordinates with Product Managers on scope trade-offs and sequencing decisions

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning, readiness, and communication of software releases so deployments are predictable, safe, and well understood by stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Define release windows and deployment readiness criteria
- Coordinate staging, production rollout, smoke testing, and verification
- Support rollback and incident mitigation planning
- Draft release communications and stakeholder notifications
- Ensure release dependencies and ownership are clear across teams

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Improve predictability and stakeholder confidence
- Support smooth transitions from delivery to production use

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment check-ins
- Stakeholder updates before and after launches
- Coordination with QA, engineering, and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to confirm releases meet acceptance criteria and quality checks
- Partners with Project Managers on timing, dependencies, and stakeholder communication
- Aligns with Product Managers on release messaging and launch impact

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads help ensure the project meets organizational and regulatory requirements while reducing risk exposure throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security controls, privacy, and compliance obligations for the project
- Identify security and risk issues early in planning and execution
- Support secure design, access controls, and incident readiness
- Validate remediation efforts and governance requirements
- Escalate high-risk issues to the appropriate stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce risk and protect customer and business assets
- Ensure compliance with policy and applicable requirements
- Embed security and governance into the delivery process

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and design conversations
- Risk assessments and escalation updates
- Cross-functional briefings during release or incident readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers and Technical Architects on secure implementation decisions
- Works with Project Managers and Product Managers to assess risk impact and mitigation plans
- Supports stakeholder communication when security or compliance concerns require action

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Stakeholder Liaisons represent the voice of users, customers, and key stakeholders to ensure that delivery decisions remain informed by practical needs and adoption outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder feedback and voice-of-customer insights
- Support adoption planning, launch readiness, and post-release feedback loops
- Surface unmet needs, service issues, and stakeholder concerns
- Help translate customer needs into product and project priorities
- Coordinate communication between project teams and customer-facing groups

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption outcomes
- Ensure stakeholder needs inform delivery decisions
- Strengthen alignment between project execution and real-world value delivery

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and feedback sessions
- Customer-facing readiness and launch briefings
- Relationship management between delivery teams and business stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides input to Product Managers on unmet needs and value delivery
- Shares customer insights with Developers and Project Managers to inform scope and prioritization
- Supports communication with stakeholders and sponsors during milestones and launches

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams define and measure success, enabling evidence-based decisions about product performance, operational health, and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define key metrics, reporting needs, and dashboards for the project
- Support instrumentation, measurement, and success evaluation
- Analyze trends in adoption, quality, performance, and user behavior
- Partner with the team to interpret outcomes and identify improvement opportunities
- Help connect project results to business and user value

### Goals
- Improve decision quality with measurable evidence
- Make outcomes visible and actionable for stakeholders
- Support continuous improvement based on observed results

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews and reporting updates
- Performance analysis with Product and PM stakeholders
- Insights shared across planning and retrospective cycles

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers on success metrics and outcome tracking
- Supports Project Managers in status reporting and risk monitoring
- Helps Developers and QA validate feature quality and user impact through measurable signals

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify accountability, ownership, and collaboration across the full lifecycle of a project.
